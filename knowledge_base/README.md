# Knowledge base

This folder describes how I plan to build the evidence corpus. No documents have been ingested yet.

## Sources

I plan to use clinical guidelines, systematic reviews and relevant research articles on uterine fibroids. The aim is to cover classification, imaging, treatment and fertility, while keeping track of the population and context of each source.

For each source I will record its title, URL, DOI or PMID, date, version, licence, source type, population, jurisdiction and available evidence-quality information. I will also keep a hash to identify the exact version used.

## Preparing the corpus

- Remove duplicates and check corrections or retractions.
- Store source metadata separately from the text.
- Use only text that I have permission to reuse; keep links only when reuse is not allowed.
- Split allowed text into passages with IDs and offsets so answers can be traced back to the source.
- Prepare a small French/English glossary for medical terms and synonyms.

The pilot search helped identify possible sources, but final selection will happen during the literature review. The candidate source IDs in the evaluation CSV are provisional; see [candidate references](../review/candidate_references.csv) and [bibliography](../review/references.bib).

I will not collect patient data. Any local studies will be interpreted in their own context, not as proof of a national medical policy.
